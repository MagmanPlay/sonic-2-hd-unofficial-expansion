# Unofficial Expansion Patch v1

For Sonic 2 HD Demo 2.0 (Windows build 1012), by MagmanPlay.

Team S2HD made the demo that started this project. I wanted to find out what it would feel like to leave Chemical Plant, head straight into Aquatic Ruin, and eventually take the long way down into Mystic Cave. This patch adds both zones to the Windows demo. Each has two acts and its own boss, with the routes laid out around the 1992 Mega Drive game.

Aquatic Ruin lets you stay high among the pillars or take your chances underwater. Those routes meet up again, so missing a jump doesn't have to end a run. Both acts use eph's Aquatic Ruin Act 1 preview arrangement. In Mystic Cave, the vines, mine bridges, moving crates and spike traps lead down to Eggman's drill machine. The cave uses Tee Lopes' Mystic Cave arrangement. Both zones have new HD scenery and objects drawn to fit alongside the rest of the demo.

## What changed in v1

- The campaign now runs through Emerald Hill, Chemical Plant, Aquatic Ruin, Hill Top and Mystic Cave, in that order. The two additions bring the total to five playable zones.
- Aquatic Ruin's slopes, loops and springs were checked on foot and in a roll. I fixed spots where Sonic could catch on a seam, shoot out of a submerged slope or roll through a wall. Moving blocks now support him at their visible tops, and approaching a falling pillar from the side no longer counts as getting crushed underneath it.
- I adjusted checkpoints, monitors and column bases that sat above or below the floor. Grounders respect solid obstacles, and a broken wall opens onto the ruin behind it. Air bubbles, the drowning countdown and the splash when you surface were reworked. The underwater backdrop extends into the deeper rooms, and the water level changes gradually between acts.
- In Mystic Cave, I reworked the mine rails, timber supports, lanterns and cave walls. Sonic and Tails can release a vine into the adjoining passage. The moving crates before the spike pit keep their spacing.
- Egg Hammer's pillars mark the edges of the arena. The machine hovers above an ordinary jump from the floor, so the pillars and arrows matter. Its hammer swings around the cockpit when it turns, and its exhaust flames line up with the nozzles. Egg Digger's falling debris now reaches both sides of the cave. Its underside thruster fires on the descent, lift and escape. Across all five boss fights, Eggman laughs when Sonic takes a hit and holds position if Sonic loses a life.
- At the end of an act the camera follows a fast finish past the signpost and frames the capsule after a boss. I also fixed a case where Sonic and Tails could fall through the scenery as the next act's title card appeared.
- The Video menu can follow your display's refresh rate or use a rate you select. The game still updates its physics at 60 ticks per second. Characters and moving platforms draw between those ticks on faster displays, and pausing holds them in place.
- Speed Shoes pick up the music at the same point in the faster track, then return to the regular tempo when they wear off. You can press Enter to finish the bonus tally, and the Game menu has a Roll Jump Lock option.
